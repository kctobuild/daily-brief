Top Client Question of the Day
▾

"With September rate-cut odds essentially gone after Warsh's hawkish debut and the 2Y now above 4.3%, should we finally add front-end duration, or does the flatter curve argue for staying short?"

House view

Add at the front end now rather than wait. A 2Y above 4.3% with cut odds off the table is an attractive risk-adjusted carry level that may not last if data softens into September's payrolls print. The curve flattened, not steepened, on Friday's move — 2s10s at just 38bp means extending into 10Y buys little incremental yield for a lot more rate risk. Scale into 2-3Y paper for the carry; hold off on further duration extension until the curve re-steepens.
