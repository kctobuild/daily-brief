"Markets are closed until Monday, and next week brings the FOMC — should clients use the weekend to reposition ahead of the decision, or wait for the outcome?"

House view

Use the weekend to prepare, not to pre-commit. With a hike now ~85-90% priced after Friday's back-up to 4.95% on the 10Y, the surprise risk sits with the tone, not the move itself — a hawkish hold would hurt more than a well-flagged hike. We'd stage duration additions in tranches after Wednesday's statement rather than front-run Monday's open, and keep the collapsed VIX (15.84) in mind: cheaper vol argues for locking coupon on new autocallables before the FOMC reprices it higher.
