Top Client Question of the Day
▾

"VIX just fell to a 14-handle even as the MOVE index sits near its highest since March — is the equity market underpricing risk here?"

House view

No — this is a genuine cross-asset divergence, not equity complacency. The MOVE spike is a rates-specific story, a fast repricing of duration risk after the 10-year broke above 5%, and equities haven't needed to reprice because credit hasn't deteriorated — IG and HY spreads are only 2bp and 7bp wider. Use the low VIX print to add optionality cheaply now, before rate volatility eventually bleeds into equities, rather than treating 14.87 as a signal to cut hedges.
