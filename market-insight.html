Top Client Question of the Day
▾

"Yields haven't actually come down yet — should we still be adding duration now, or wait until the Fed confirms a hold?"

House view

We would start adding now rather than wait for confirmation. Fed Governor Waller's remarks Thursday were the clearest dovish signal from a sitting official this cycle, and at 4.79% the 10Y is still close to its highest level since November 2023 — the risk to yields from here is asymmetric to the downside. The 40bp 2s10s spread offers modest but real duration pickup, and VIX's drop into the Low regime lowers the cost of hedging the position. Waiting for a formal hold risks missing the entry.
