Top client question of the day

Should I roll my maturing tech autocallable while implied vol is higher?

Take the proceeds and reinvest in stages. The higher volatility can improve the headline coupon, but it also raises the risk of owning the worst performer after another leg down. For clients with existing semiconductor exposure, redeploy a first tranche into short-dated USD IG and use a smaller broad-index note for the equity allocation. Compare downside barriers at the same tenor before committing the balance.
