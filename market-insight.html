Top Client Question of the Day
▾

"Oil just jumped another 6-8% and the Nasdaq 100 is down over 1% — should we be trimming equity risk into the FOMC, or is this move overdone?"

House view

Don't trim core equity wholesale, but do de-risk the highest-beta, rate-sensitive tech sleeve — that's where the 4.83% 10Y and 40bp 2s10s are doing the damage. The oil move is fresh and headline-driven, not yet confirmed as durable, so chasing energy broadly is premature; a laddered, buffered entry via autocallables captures upside without concentrating timing risk. Hold the line on duration until the September 15-16 FOMC clarifies the rate path — that's the real catalyst, not this week's oil spike.
