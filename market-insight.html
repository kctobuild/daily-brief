Top Client Question of the Day
▾

"Yields are at cycle highs and equities are wobbling into today's US CPI print — do we lock in autocallable coupons now, or wait for the data before committing fresh capital?"

House view

Layer in now rather than wait. The move to 4.81% on the 10Y is a supply-side, Iran-driven repricing, not the wage and services inflation the CPI print will actually test — a benign read would likely see yields ease back and today's elevated coupon levels disappear with them. We would commit a first tranche into shorter-dated autocalls at current strikes and hold a reserve to add opportunistically only if CPI surprises hot and pushes yields further out.
