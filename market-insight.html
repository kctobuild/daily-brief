Regional Outlook — Asia / Hong Kong
▾

Hong Kong and China extended Thursday's bond-relief rally: the Hang Seng added 1.06% to 25,495 and HSCEI 0.90% to 8,547.84, with CSI 300 up 0.57% as onshore sentiment held firm. Southbound buying remains the structural bid behind the Hong Kong rally. With US yields ticking back up overnight, we'd keep client conversations anchored on the mainland policy and flow story rather than US rate noise.

Top Client Question of the Day
▾

"The 10-year is back above 4.7% and the Treasury rally has fizzled — is now the time to add duration, or stay short and ride the HK/China equity rally instead?"

House view

Don't chase the long end yet — this week's rout shows the back end is still hostage to heavy Treasury supply and buyback-policy uncertainty, so keep fixed income duration in the front end and belly where the modestly positive 2s10s (+50bp) already pays you to wait. On the equity sleeve, stay overweight HK/China: the bond-relief bounce, a stable HKD peg and low China yields all argue the southbound bid has further to run. Use structured carry — autocallables on rates, participation notes on HSI/HSCEI — rather than outright duration extension.
