Top Client Question of the Day
▾

"The Fed hikes today — do we add duration now, or wait for the statement and press conference?"

House view

Don't wait for the print. A 25bp move to 3.75-4.00% is priced at close to 90%, so the hike itself is unlikely to be the surprise — the dot plot and Chair Warsh's tone on further tightening will move the belly of the curve more than the decision does. With 10Y near 4.97% and 2s10s compressed to 32bp, we'd start scaling into intermediate duration into any post-meeting yield spike, rather than chasing a rally if the tone turns dovish.
