Regional Outlook — Asia / Hong Kong
▾

Hong Kong extended its rally again today — the Hang Seng up 1.2% to around 26,000 and HSCEI up a similar margin — on softer local inflation and relentless southbound buying: a single-day net inflow of HK$35.9bn earlier this month pushed year-to-date southbound flows past HK$938.9bn, already well above all of 2025. HIBOR has eased sharply this year, a tailwind for property refinancing conversations, though we don't have a fresh print to quote today. With mainland policy support still the dominant driver, keep client conversations anchored on China catalysts rather than global rate noise.

Top Client Question of the Day
▾

"Hang Seng just hit fresh highs on record southbound inflows while the US 10-year is still near a 20-month high — should we lean harder into HK/China equities and trim duration on the fixed income sleeve?"

House view

Yes to leaning into HK/China — the southbound bid is structural, not a one-day flow, and mainland policy support plus softer local inflation give the rally a fundamental underpin, not just momentum. On rates, we would not chase the long end after this week's spike; front-end and belly carry stay attractive without taking on duration risk. Use structured carry — autocallables or range accruals on rates — to capture the elevated coupon while credit spreads remain this tight, rather than extending duration outright.
