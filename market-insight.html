Regional Outlook — Asia / Hong Kong
▾

Hong Kong steadied after Monday's Alibaba-driven wobble, the Hang Seng little changed at 25,511 (-0.02%) and HSCEI off a modest 0.31% to 8,445, as Alibaba itself rebounded 0.7% even as Meituan fell 3%. August southbound inflows remain historic — over HK$938bn year-to-date, already topping all of 2025 — underpinning the tech complex through the single-stock noise. Focus now shifts to Friday's Jackson Hole speech, Fed Chair Warsh's first, with roughly one-in-three odds of a September hike being discussed.

Top Client Question of the Day
▾

"Markets are pricing roughly one-in-three odds the Fed hikes, not cuts, in September — should we be repositioning our rate view before Friday?"

House view

Not yet. That pricing reflects the Iran-driven energy shock more than a durable inflation regime change, and Tuesday's 3-5% pullback in Brent and WTI — as Washington pivots to economic-pressure sanctions rather than further escalation — already argues that shock is fading. We'd hold current duration and wait for Chair Warsh's tone at Jackson Hole Friday rather than de-risk into a single data point. A confirmed hawkish pivot would be the trigger to shorten, not this week's pricing alone.
