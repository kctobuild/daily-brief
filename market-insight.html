Regional Outlook — Asia / Hong Kong
▾

Markets are closed for the weekend; Friday was a quiet, directionless finish in Hong Kong even as Wall Street wobbled on Warsh's hawkish inflation warning — Hang Seng +0.07% to 25,584.79, HSCEI flat at 8,490.39, CSI 300 -0.46%. USD/HKD held 7.8402, comfortably inside the peg band. Southbound flows remain the standout: a record HK$35.9bn single-day inflow on 15 August has pushed year-to-date net buying to HK$938.9bn, already well past all of last year's HK$807.9bn, a structural bid under HK-listed China names. HIBOR's expected easing path into year-end — a swing factor for property financing costs — is the one to watch if Friday's hawkish tone proves to have legs into next week.

Top Client Question of the Day
▾

"Gold just had its worst session in months after Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole remarks — do we trim into strength before more downside, or is this a buying opportunity for clients still underweight the metal?"

House view

Use the pullback to add rather than chase it lower. A single hawkish speech doesn't reverse the structural case for gold — diversification against fiscal and rate-path uncertainty is intact, and a 3%+ single-day move looks more like a positioning flush than a regime change. For clients still underweight, scale in on this dip; for those already full, sit tight rather than adding into the immediate air-pocket and let Monday's Asia reaction confirm direction first.
