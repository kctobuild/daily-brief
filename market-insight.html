Structured Product Read-Through

The 10-year's push to 5.11% (FRED) — a fresh post-2007 high — keeps outright yields elevated enough to underpin rich coupon levels on new note issuance, even though the VIX's modest 3.23% rise to 15.67 leaves implied vol still inside the Normal band rather than genuinely cheap. Regional Outlook — Asia / Hong Kong
▾

Hong Kong's Thursday session was directionless: the Hang Seng eased 0.29% to 24,761.13 and the HSCEI slipped 0.09% to 8,266.01, while the CSI 300 fell a sharper 1.73% to 4,439.14 as mainland investors digested a Trump-Xi summit that delivered only a two-month trade-truce extension to 10 January rather than the broader deal some had hoped for. USD/HKD held firm at 7.8425, inside the peg, and China's 10-year stayed anchored at 1.672% even as US yields spiked to a post-2007 high. Expect cautious positioning into the weekend as clients weigh whether the summit outcome is a floor or a delay.
