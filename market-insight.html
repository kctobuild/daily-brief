Top Client Question of the Day
▾

"Yields just rebounded sharply and gold broke $4,500 — should we be adding duration here, or sitting in cash?"

House view

We are not chasing duration into this rebound. Front-end and belly carry remain attractive without taking on long-end volatility risk, and the Treasury's buyback programme is a floor for the long end, not a reason to extend into it. For clients wanting rate exposure, structured carry — range accruals or autocallables referencing rates — captures the elevated coupon without outright duration risk. Keep gold and broader commodity exposure as the geopolitical hedge rather than adding conviction long-duration bond risk today.
