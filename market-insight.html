Regional Outlook — Asia / Hong Kong
▾

Hong Kong closed out a third straight losing session Friday, the Hang Seng down 1.01% to 24,510 as NetEase, Xiaomi and JD.com led a tech-heavy retreat. Mainland exchanges are shut Friday through Sunday for the Mid-Autumn Festival and Golden Week, with Stock Connect flows paused and the CSI 300 frozen at Thursday's 4,439 print until Monday's reopen. USD/HKD held at 7.8435, comfortably inside the peg, and China's 10-year stayed anchored near 1.68% even as US yields spiked to a fresh high. Expect thin liquidity and headline-driven swings into Monday's mainland return.

Top Client Question of the Day
▾

"The Hang Seng just closed its third straight down session heading into a weekend where mainland markets are shut for Mid-Autumn — should we trim HK tech exposure before Monday?"

House view

No — treat this as thin, holiday-driven de-risking rather than a trend change. Stock Connect southbound flows are paused, not reversed, and the pullback is concentrated in a handful of large-cap tech names rather than broad-based. USD/HKD at 7.8435 and China's 10-year near 1.68% show no funding or growth stress. Use Monday's mainland reopen as the read-through moment — if the CSI 300 stabilises above 4,400, add back HK tech on weakness rather than trimming into it.
