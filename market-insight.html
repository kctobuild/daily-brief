Regional Outlook — Asia / Hong Kong
▾

Hong Kong and mainland markets are closed for the weekend after a firm finish Friday, with the Hang Seng adding 1.21% to 26,009 and CSI 300 up 0.57%. Southbound flows remain the dominant technical story — a record HK$35.9bn single-day net buy earlier this month has pushed year-to-date inflows past HK$938bn, well above all of last year, as mainland households rotate out of a property market still down double digits on investment. USD/HKD stayed comfortably inside the peg band and China's 10-year held near 1.69%. Into Monday, we'd frame client conversations around HSI momentum and the durability of southbound demand rather than any single print, watching for policy signals out of Beijing over the rest of the weekend.
