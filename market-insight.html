Top Client Question of the Day
▾

"Nvidia reports after tonight's close and July's PCE just came in hotter than expected — should we cut risk before the print?"

House view

No — trim single-name concentration where a client is overweight one name, but don't de-risk the broader book. Hotter PCE mainly repriced the pace of cuts, not the direction, and the parallel drift lower in both the 10-year and 2-year (-6bp/-7bp) shows the market isn't pricing a growth scare. Nvidia's print will move implied vol on AI-linked structures either way — that argues for using the pre-earnings vol to write yield-enhancement notes, not for stepping to the sidelines.
