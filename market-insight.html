Top Client Question of the Day
▾

"Yields just hit a 2007 high and stocks sold off hard — is this the start of a real correction, or a buying opportunity for duration?"

House view

Treat it as a repricing, not a regime change. The move is being driven by hawkish Fed rhetoric and stronger PMI data, not a credit event — IG spreads are flat at 77bp and HY only 2bp wider, so risk markets aren't flagging stress. With 2s10s back out to 29bp, the belly-to-long end offers the best entry in weeks. Ladder into 7-10y exposure rather than going all-in, and keep dry powder in case the October Fed decision extends the sell-off.
