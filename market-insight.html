Top Client Question of the Day
▾

"We laid into autocallable exposure yesterday near the yield highs — now that the 10-year has eased and VIX has dropped almost 7%, have we missed the best entry, or is there room to add the reserve tranche?"

House view

No — Wednesday's move was a breather, not a reversal. NY Fed's Williams framed the surge in yields as economy-driven rather than dysfunction, and Brent/WTI's fresh 1% gains on Hormuz risk show the geopolitical premium underpinning vol hasn't gone away. At 4.78% the 10Y is still near cycle highs, so yesterday's tranche remains well-timed. We would hold the reserve rather than chase today's calmer VIX print — Asia's risk-off tone, with Nikkei -2.60% and CSI 300 -1.38%, points to more two-way volatility ahead.
