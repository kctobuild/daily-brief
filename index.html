China's new bank loans contract by a record as borrowers shun debt

▾
Bloomberg

Chinese borrowers net repaid 590 billion yuan of loans in July, the largest contraction on record, as households and businesses pulled back from new debt. Overall credit, including interbank lending, shrank by 340 billion yuan, more than triple what economists expected. Government bond issuance failed to offset the slump in private borrowing.

Why it matters
  • A record loan contraction signals weak confidence among Chinese households and firms, raising pressure on Beijing for stronger stimulus.
  • Shrinking bank credit growth squeezes lending margins and asset quality at Chinese banks already facing a prolonged property downturn.
  • Persistently soft mainland credit demand ripples into Hong Kong's banking sector and regional trade financing tied to China.
