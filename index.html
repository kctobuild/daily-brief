US economic growth cools to 1.5% as inflation pressure eases

▾
CNBC

US GDP grew just 1.5% in the second quarter, missing the 1.8% forecast economists had penciled in.   • A slower US economy can dampen demand for Asian exports, a risk factor for China and Hong Kong-listed exporters.
  • Banks are recalibrating rate-cut timelines, which affects net interest margins across US and Hong Kong lenders alike.
