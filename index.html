Nvidia, Wall Street asset managers unveil $500 billion plan to treat AI compute as a financeable asset class

▾
CNBC

Nvidia is teaming up with major Wall Street asset managers on plans to treat AI computing capacity as an investable asset class, similar to how mortgage lenders assess homes. The push aims to unlock roughly $500 billion in outside capital for data-center buildouts. It comes as demand for AI compute keeps outstripping available supply.

Why it matters
  • Treating AI compute like real estate could open a huge new pool of institutional capital for banks and asset managers to underwrite.
  • Financing AI infrastructure through securitized structures spreads risk beyond tech balance sheets and into the broader credit market.
  • Success would deepen Wall Street's role as the financial backbone of the AI buildout, reshaping how banks price data-center risk.
